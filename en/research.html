Research

My research is in partial differential equations, with a particular interest in problems related to conservation laws. I am currently interested in the stability of traveling waves for viscous conservation laws and in the interaction between shock waves and boundaries.

Research Interests

  • Partial Differential Equations
  • Conservation Laws
  • Viscous Shock Waves and Boundary Layers
  • Partial Differential Equations in Fluid Dynamics
  • Singular Perturbation Problems

Current Research

I am currently studying the asymptotic stability of traveling waves for viscous conservation laws on a half-line. In particular, I am interested in the behavior of viscous shock waves propagating toward a boundary and in the transition to a different asymptotic state through their interaction with the boundary.

Recently, I have been studying the viscous Burgers equation as a model problem, focusing on the transition of an incoming viscous shock wave to a boundary layer. I am also interested in extending this analysis to more general conservation laws and fluid equations.
